## Critical Findings from Market Research

### 1. GCC Job Market Reality
**Salaries (Junior, 0-3 years):**
| Country | Monthly (local) | Monthly (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| UAE | AED 10,000–18,000 | $2,700–$4,900 |
| Saudi | SAR 8,000–14,000 | $2,100–$3,700 |
| Qatar | QAR 6,000–10,000 | $1,600–$2,700 |
| Oman | OMR 600–1,200 | $1,500–$3,100 |

**Important:** Gulf employers evaluate against **unique criteria:**
- Extreme heat affects equipment ratings, cable sizing, switchgear
- Must know local standards: **DEWA (Dubai), SERA (Saudi), ADDC (Abu Dhabi), Qatar General Electricity**
- **Not knowing these = automatic rejection**

### 2. Local Registration (Critical for Career Growth)
| Country | Body | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Saudi Arabia | **Saudi Council of Engineers (SCE)** | Required for senior roles |
| UAE | **UAE Society of Engineers** | Design sign-off authority |
| Qatar | **QEAC** | Engineer registration |
| Kuwait | **Kuwait Society of Engineers** | Required for progression |

**Action:** Register with SCE or UAE Society immediately after arrival.
